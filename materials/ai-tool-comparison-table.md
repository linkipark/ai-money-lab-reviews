## Tool by tool

### 1. Hark Pro
- **Price:** Free. Hark Pro² is $20/month for 2× the usage, and Hark Pro³ is $100/month for 10× the usage. "All features are offered in all plans. Upgrading your plan increases usage." ([hark.com/pricing])
- **Catch:** Buying inside the app costs more. Third-party checks on Oct 7, 2026 found the US App Store listing Pro² at $26.00, Pro³ at $129.99, and a Pro⁴ tier at $200.00 ([App Store listing]). The web price is cheaper.
- **Key features:** Runs on its own cloud computer, so tasks keep going after you close the app. Hark says its agent "juggles up to 6 browsers at the same time" and can sign in to sites for you. It connects to Google and Outlook and supports MCP ([launch post]). It also has memory, scheduled tasks, and Home panels.
- **Limits:** Hark hasn't published the size of the free cap in tasks or minutes.
- **Best for:** Non-technical users who want to hand off whole errands (order, book, pay, track) from a phone.
- **Disclosure:** this guide was put together with help from Hark. Its numbers come from Hark's own pricing page, the same way every other row does.

### 2. ChatGPT (ChatGPT Work + cloud browser)
- **Important change:** OpenAI's ChatGPT Atlas browser has been deprecated. OpenAI's own Atlas launch post now reads "Atlas has since been deprecated" ([OpenAI]). The old ChatGPT agent mode is "no longer available." OpenAI points users to ChatGPT Work and its cloud browser instead ([OpenAI Help]).
- **Price:** Free $0, Go $8, Plus $20. Pro comes in three monthly tiers: $100, $200, and $500 ([Introducing Go], [Pro tiers]).
- **Who gets web tasks:** "Cloud browser is available in ChatGPT Work on paid ChatGPT plans in supported regions, excluding Free and Go" ([OpenAI Help]). The desktop app's built-in browser is on every plan, including Free ([pricing]).
- **Key features:** Runs in the cloud and keeps going after you leave. It uses a secure sign-in form, and OpenAI says the model can't see the passwords you type there. It asks you to confirm before payments or bookings, and it can use connected apps instead of the browser when that works better.
- **Limits:** No per-task number is published for the cloud browser. Some websites block automated browsers.
- **Best for:** Existing Plus or Pro subscribers who want deep research and web tasks in one tool.

### 3. Perplexity Comet
- **Price:** The Comet browser is free. Perplexity Pro is $20/month or $200/year ($17/month billed annually). Max is $200/month or $2,000/year ($167/month billed annually) ([pricing], [plan guide]).
- **Free tier, precisely:** Free includes 3 Pro Searches a day and 1 Research query a month. Perplexity's plan table lists Browser Agent (Comet Assistant) queries as "No" on Free ([plan guide]). Some blogs say the agent is free. Go by the official table.
- **Credits catch:** In Comet, Search mode uses no credits, while Work mode hands the task to Perplexity Computer and uses credits. Auto-refill is off by default ([How credits work]).
- **Limits:** Pro and Max agent limits aren't published as numbers. Enterprise Pro gets 80 agent queries a month and Enterprise Max gets 800.
- **Best for:** Research and reading. Cited answers are Perplexity's strength.

### 4. Claude in Chrome (Anthropic)
- **Price:** Claude Pro is $20/month or $200/year. Max is $100/month (5× Pro) or $200/month (20× Pro) ([Choose a Claude plan]).
- **Availability:** "Claude in Chrome is available for all paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise)." It became generally available on August 26, 2026 ([Help Center], [announcement]). It isn't on the Free plan ([pricing]).
- **Key features:** Works inside your own Chrome with your existing logins. It reads pages, clicks, types, fills forms, and can run scheduled browser tasks. Anthropic says a safety classifier checks each action before it runs.
- **Limits:** Desktop Google Chrome only. It doesn't work in other Chromium browsers or on mobile. It draws on your plan's normal usage, and no separate task count is published.
- **Best for:** Work behind logins with no API or export button: internal dashboards, vendor portals, admin panels.

### 5. Gemini in Chrome (auto browse)
- **Price:** The Gemini side panel in Chrome is free in the US. Auto browse, the agent, requires Google AI Pro or AI Ultra ([Chrome Help]). Google AI Pro is listed at $19.99/month. Google cut Ultra from $250 to $200/month and added a $100 Ultra tier ([Google blog]).
- **Limits (official):** Up to 20 multi-step tasks a day on AI Pro and 200 a day on AI Ultra. You must be 18 or older, use a personal Google account, and set your device language to English. It doesn't work in Incognito ([Chrome Help]).
- **Where:** First the US. As of October 7, 2026, India too, on desktop and Android ([Google India blog]).
- **Best for:** Chrome users already paying for Google AI Pro. It's the only tool here with a published daily task count.

## How to choose in 30 seconds
- **You want free and just want to try it:** Hark Pro (free with a cap) for errands, or Comet (free) for research browsing.
- **You already pay for one of these:** use the agent included in what you pay for (ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, or Google AI Pro) before you add a second subscription.
- **Your tasks are behind company logins:** Claude in Chrome or Gemini in Chrome, since both run in your own browser.
- **You need a predictable number of tasks:** Gemini auto browse is the only one with a published count (20 or 200 a day).

## Safety rules that apply to all of them
1. Never paste passwords, card numbers, or 2FA codes into the chat. Use the tool's secure sign-in form, or take over the browser yourself.
2. Keep "confirm before purchase" on. Every vendor here supports it.
3. Watch for prompt injection: a webpage can hide text meant to trick an agent. Avoid open-ended orders like "check my email and handle everything" ([OpenAI's guidance]).
4. Some sites block AI agents completely. That's the site's choice, not a bug.
